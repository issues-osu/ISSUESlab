## Upcoming Presentations & Talks

### IPR Seminar Series: Abortion Accessibility & Maternal Health

**March 10, 2026 | 12:30-1:30 PM | Townshend Hall & Zoom**

Dr. Gia Barboza-Salerno will present on "Accessibility to Abortion Services, Maternal Vulnerability, and Birth Outcomes" at the Institute for Population Research Seminar Series. The talk examines how spatial constraints on abortion access align with community-level maternal vulnerability and adverse birth outcomes using a novel Maternal Vulnerability Index and GIS-based transportation network modeling.
