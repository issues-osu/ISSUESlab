Zahra Alhajjaj is a PhD candidate in Nursing at The Ohio State University with a background in oncology nursing and clinical practice. Her research focuses on HPV vaccination among Muslim mothers, particularly the role of health beliefs, knowledge, and other factors influencing vaccination decisions. She is passionate about nursing education, evidence-based practice, and culturally sensitive healthcare. She is also interested in teaching and supporting nursing students as they develop their clinical knowledge, skills, and professional confidence.
