Dr. Keith Warren, Dr. Gia Barboza-Salerno, and Taylor Harrington will present at the **2026 Ohio Recovery Housing Conference** during Breakout Session D (1:00 - 2:15 PM) on Wednesday, April 15, 2026.

This presentation will share findings from the lab's ongoing research on recovery housing, examining the role of recovery housing in supporting individuals with substance use disorder, particularly in underserved and high-risk communities.

**Conference Details:**

- **Date:** April 15, 2026
- **Time:** 1:00 - 2:15 PM
- **Session:** Breakout Session D
