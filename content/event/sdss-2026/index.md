## Abstract

This research demonstrates the application of advanced natural language processing techniques—specifically Large Language Models (LLMs) and Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA)—to automatically detect and classify intimate partner violence injury mechanisms from emergency department clinical narratives.

By analyzing unstructured text data from emergency department records, this work advances automated detection methods for IPV-related injuries, which can improve clinical screening, documentation, and intervention strategies. The study contributes to the growing body of work applying machine learning and AI to public health surveillance and violence prevention.
