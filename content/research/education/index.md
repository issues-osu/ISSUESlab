  <p>Our lab explores the profound connection between neighborhood safety and educational opportunity. A major focus of our work is the impact of <strong>gun violence on youth</strong> and how exposure to community violence affects student attendance, achievement, and mental health. We also examine <strong>disciplinary injustice</strong> in schools, particularly for Native American students and other marginalized groups, investigating how exclusionary discipline policies perpetuate the school-to-prison pipeline.</p>

  <h2>Core Questions We Address</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>How does exposure to <strong>neighborhood gun violence</strong> on routes to school impact academic performance and disciplinary outcomes?</li>
    <li>What are the spatial patterns of violence near schools, and how do they respond to policy changes like Safe Passage programs?</li>
    <li>How do structural inequities like <strong>exclusionary discipline</strong> and racial segregation interact with safe neighborhood conditions?</li>
    <li>What role does geospatial analysis play in understanding school safety and identifying high-risk corridors for students?</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Methods and Approaches</h2>
  <p>We apply rigorous spatial and statistical techniques to understand the educational context:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Acoustic Detection Sensors:</strong> Utilizing <strong>ShotSpotter</strong> data to capture precise, real-time incidents of gunfire that are often underreported in administrative crime data.</li>
    <li><strong>Spatial Accessibility Analysis:</strong> Modeling the density of gun violence along student travel routes to identify high-risk corridors.</li>
    <li><strong>Geospatial Machine Learning:</strong> Using AI to analyze the urban built environment and its relationship to school safety and student outcomes.</li>
    <li><strong>Policy Evaluation:</strong> Assessing the effectiveness of <strong>Safe Passages</strong> interventions and reforms aimed at dismantling the school-to-prison pipeline.</li>
  </ul>
