  <p>Our lab adopts a comprehensive life-course perspective to investigate the <strong>manifestation of violence across the lifespan</strong>. Guided by a <strong>Big Data for Social Justice</strong> framework, we study how structural disadvantage contributes to interpersonal violence at every stage of life. A pivotal area of our research is the spatio-temporal examination of <strong>"Elder Abuse in Nursing Homes: An Ecological Perspective,"</strong> where we analyze how facility characteristics and neighborhood contexts shape safety for the elderly.</p>

  <h2>Core Questions We Address</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>How does violence manifest differently across developmental stages, from youth bullying to domestic abuse in later life?</li>
    <li>What are the <strong>spatio-temporal patterns of elder abuse</strong>, and what markers (e.g., staffing levels, neighborhood demographics) predict high-risk facilities?</li>
    <li>How do structural factors like <strong>neighborhood disinvestment</strong> and surveillance contribute to the cycle of violence across generations?</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Methods and Approaches</h2>
  <p>We utilize interdisciplinary methods to capture the complexity of violence:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Spatio-Temporal Modeling:</strong> mapping the trajectory of violence within communities and across time to identify persistent hotspots.</li>
    <li><strong>Ecological Perspectives:</strong> Analyzing violence not just as individual acts, but as outcomes of broader environmental and systemic conditions.</li>
    <li><strong>Big Data Integration:</strong> Leveraging large administrative datasets to monitor potential rates of abuse, neglect, and exploitation in vulnerable populations.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Ongoing Projects</h2>
  <p>Current studies include a longitudinal analysis of <strong>violence across the lifespan</strong>, a spatial examination of elder abuse reports in relation to community resources, and research into the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic on patterns of domestic and institutional violence. Our work aims to inform comprehensive prevention strategies that protect individuals at every age.</p>
