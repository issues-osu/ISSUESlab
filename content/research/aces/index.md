 <p>Our lab takes a nuanced approach to understanding Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs), moving beyond simple cumulative scores to examine the configuration and context of adversity. We investigate <strong>latent classes of ACEs</strong>—distinct patterns of abuse, neglect, and household dysfunction—to understand how different combinations of trauma differentially impact development. A critical area of our work is the association between these latent classes and <strong>suicide attempts</strong>, particularly among vulnerable populations such as gender-diverse youth.</p>

  <h2>Core Questions We Address</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>How do specific <strong>latent classes of ACEs</strong> (e.g., household dysfunction vs. physical abuse) relate to differing risks for delinquency and mental health outcomes?</li>
    <li>What is the cumulative impact of ACEs on long-term health, and how does this vary by race, gender, and sexual orientation?</li>
    <li>How do neighborhood structural factors interact with household-level adversity to compound risk?</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Methods and Approaches</h2>
  <p>We employ advanced statistical techniques to uncover these complex relationships:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Latent Class Analysis (LCA):</strong> Identifying subgroups of individuals with similar profiles of ACE exposure to tailor interventions.</li>
    <li><strong>Longitudinal Modeling:</strong> Examining the developmental cascade of trauma, from early childhood adversity to adolescent and adult outcomes.</li>
    <li><strong>Intersectionality Frameworks:</strong> analyzing how ACEs impact health and justice outcomes within specific marginalized populations, including LGBT individuals.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>Ongoing Projects</h2>
  <p>Current studies include a detailed examination of ACE patterns and <strong>suicide risk</strong> among adolescents, research into the link between cumulative trauma and juvenile justice involvement, and the development of screening tools that account for the diverse manifestations of childhood adversity.</p>
